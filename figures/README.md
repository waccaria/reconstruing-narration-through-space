# Figure documentation

## Paper figures

The `original/` directory preserves the available figures associated with the 2025
course project:

- Figure 2.1: semantic clusters around `tent`;
- Figure 2.2: lexical dispersion of `tent` and `battlefield`;
- Figure 2.3: noun collocations around `tent` in *The Silence of the Girls*;
- Figure 3.1: Briseis's movement in *The Silence of the Girls*;
- Figures 3.4-3.5: Achilles's movement in *The Silence of the Girls*;
- Figure 3.6: Achilles's movement in *The Iliad*.

The `revised/` directory contains cleaner exports of the surviving *Iliad* movement
figures for Briseis and Achilles. These revised versions are preferred for display.

## Missing figure material

The supplementary Briseis movement figure identified as Figure 3.2 in the paper is
not present in the recovered archive. Figure 2.4 appears in the paper PDF, but its
underlying manual KWIC annotation file has not been recovered.

## Exploratory outputs

Additional emotion and storyline graphics are preserved locally in
`figures/exploratory/`. They are excluded from the paper release because some were
not used in the submitted paper and their original parameters or scripts have not
yet been recovered. They should not be presented as reproducible final findings.
